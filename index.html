What is a DNS method?

DNS methods block the Apple servers that constantly track your device with a given certificate. Sideloading communities use them to reduce blacklist issues and extend certificate life.

FAQ
What happens if my device restarts or dies?

Restarting turns off the DNS. In those first seconds of boot before your network activates, Apple can blacklist your certificate. Keep the device on. When installing updates, enable Airplane Mode first — then reconnect after the update completes.

Can I use a VPN alongside the DNS?

Most VPNs override and disable the DNS even when it appears active in your settings. It's safest to avoid VPNs while using the DNS method.

Can I use a different DNS provider?

Other providers have different configurations. The DNS available here has been extensively tested and consistently performs as intended. Stick with it.
